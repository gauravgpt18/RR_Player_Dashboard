Rajasthan Royals IPL 2027 Auction Strategy

In our dataset analysis, Rajasthan Royals' death-over batting dropped by 30% from 824 runs (54 sixes) in IPL 2022 to 563 runs (34 sixes) in IPL 2025 and 611 runs (38 sixes) in IPL 2026, while death-over wickets fell from 39 wickets to 30 wickets.

  • Middle-Order & Leadership Target: Namboori Tilak Varma (#1), Suryakumar Yadav (#2), or Rishabh Pant (#4) bring +56 to +71.5 Strike Rate acceleration and calm chase control.
  • No. 6 Death Finisher Target: Tim David (#3, 93 pressure sixes) or Rinku Singh (#8, 1,129 pressure runs) bridges RR's 2.16% death-over six-rate gap behind #1 Punjab Kings.
  • Death Pace & Spin Spearheads: Jasprit Bumrah (#1, saves +3.00 runs/over), Matheesha Pathirana (#4, 48 death wickets), and Rashid Khan (#3) / Yuzvendra Chahal (#10) restore late-innings wicket pressure.

Three Data Cleaning & Methodology Safeguards

  • 1. Removed extras1_runs Lookup Code: In ball_by_ball.csv, extras1_runs is an integer category code for extras_type (6 = No Extra, 2 = Wide, 3 = Leg Bye). Removing it prevented 1.16 million false runs.
  • 2. Separated Identical Names via Player ID: Grouped by striker_id and bowler_id so Afghanistan/GT's Rashid Khan (ID 4722) is never mixed with Nepal's Rashid Khan (ID 20172).
  • 3. IPL-Active Eligibility & Over Par Adjustment: Every player is benchmarked against Over × Format Par and their own Normal Baseline (Score < 60), with 0.99 Spearman rank stability.
